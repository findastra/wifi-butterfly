## For the Friendly Farmer (Cage registration)

The art already exists, so **don't generate it again**:

1. Copy `art/mood-sheet-20261009.png` from this repo to `art/sheets/wifi-butterfly-20261009.png` in astras-pet-apps.
2. Add this line to `PETS` in `scripts/generate-pets.mjs`, so a later `--match` redo keeps the same monarch:

   ```js
   { id: 'wifi-butterfly', what: 'a small round friendly MONARCH butterfly seen face on, with four broad rounded wings spread open in the classic monarch pattern: bold black veins and thick black wing borders dotted with small white spots, the black veins on each wing curving into three concentric arcs like a wifi signal icon, a short plump black body with white dots and two curled black antennae each tipped with a tiny glowing dot, no props and no lettering', colours: 'vivid neon flame-orange wings, bold black veins and borders, crisp white dots, black body, glowing white antenna tips' },
   ```

3. Add the `pets.json` entry with `art: "wifi-butterfly"`, `kind: "bug"`, `asset_date: "20261009"`, `visibility: "private"`, then `npm run build`.

The palette is distinct from the Goldfish's: it has no pink, and it adds black and white.
